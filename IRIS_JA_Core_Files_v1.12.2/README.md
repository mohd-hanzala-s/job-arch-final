## `IRIS_JA_6_Process_Explainer.xlsx` — the whole story on one sheet, plus a Role Grid (no names)

**How It Works** (one sheet, top to bottom): what we did step by step and the key numbers; how a job gets its level — IRIS as a building with 12 floors, five judges who each hold up a floor number (the rating) — with four grids: **Grid 1** the answer grid (what each rating 1–12 means for each of the five questions; the same grid for every job and band), **Grid 2** the weights grid (how much each question counts in each band; a rating 1 higher adds exactly the weight), **Grid 3** the same question across every band (the ratings people really received in each band and the weight it carried), **Grid 4** twelve example jobs from floor 1 to floor 12 (rating × weight = points; add up = the floor); how it is assigned to each person; the fair-play checks; filling the gaps; what happens next; how it helps IRIS; words to know.

**Role Grid**: one row per role at IRIS (same family, sub-family, designation, track, level and weight row) — the role's average rating × weight = points for each question, the score, the score's floor, and the rule when the final level differs. Filterable.


## Weightage Matrix (v1.12) — HR Super Master 1d · Product Masters 1d · Simplified files 6

One editable row of six weights per department × role group. **Settings** at the top set the Experience & Qualification weight for Bands A–B and Bands C–E. **The matrix**: yellow = the weights you enter; grey "Used" columns = the weights actually applied (Experience exactly as set, the other five scaled to fill 100%). Sheet 2 looks its weights up here, so a change recalculates points, the score and "Level the score indicates" straight away; the final level, with the fair-play rules, is confirmed after HR imports the file and rebuilds. Below the matrix: what each factor measures and when to weight it more, what a weight means (0% ignored … 35%+ dominant), rules of thumb, and the 1–12 rating grid for all six factors. Managers can overwrite a person's **Business Impact rating** on sheet 2 (yellow).

## Weight Benchmark (v1.12) — HR Super Master and Product Masters 1e · Framework & Model Master 21

Where the default weights come from: (0) the derivation — each role group is the average of Korn Ferry Hay (verified point evaluations by level), Mercer IPE (published point table) and the public plans, with the mapping rules; (1) the published point-factor plans — NHS Agenda for Change (NHS JE Handbook ch. 6), UK local-government 13-factor scheme (Police Staff Council version of the NJC scheme), GLPC, Scottish Joint Council and US OPM Factor Evaluation System — each factor's share of points, mapped to the IRIS factors; (2) the Korn Ferry Hay profile, showing know-how falling and accountability rising with seniority; (3) the evidence that experience is not scored separately (Hay, Mercer IPE, WTW) and the skills-first research (McKinsey, Deloitte, WEF, EY India 2026); (4) how the methods measure business impact; (5) IRIS's defaults against the plans' average and range; (6) the title decisions — no Director, no Fellow; (7) the limits of the benchmark — vendors keep their weights proprietary.
